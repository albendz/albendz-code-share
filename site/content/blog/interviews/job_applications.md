# Applying for Jobs

This page describes how I found and applied for jobs, tracked applications, and evaluated where to apply. I was not job hunting for an extended period of time so there
are a lot of tools and sites I didn't end up using.

This is broken up into sections:
1. Finding jobs to apply to
2. Choosing companies to apply to
3. Applying to jobs
4. What to expect after applying

## Finding jobs to apply to

I'm splitting this into to ways to find jobs: seeking out jobs on your own and working with recruiters.

### Seeking out jobs on your own

I primarily used LinkedIn to find job openings. I set up a few job alerts

I have a small list of companies I know I want to work for so I can proactively check
their careers page to see what openings they have.
