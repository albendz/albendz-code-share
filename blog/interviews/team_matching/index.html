Team Matching

This article is an FAQ guide to the team matching process in the technical hiring process for software engineers.

What is Team Matching?

Team Matching is a phase of the interview process where you meet with different hiring managers who have openings matching the level you are interviewing for. Managers will ask you questions about your goals and experience to assess whether you are a good fit for the team and if the team is a good fit for you. You will meet with one or more managers throughout this phase.

Team matching isn't considered an interview because there is no skill assessment but you are going to be pitching yourself as a teammate to potential managers.

When does Team Matching happen?

Team matching happens after the completion of the technical interview rounds and before the offer phase. An offer is not guaranteed during team matching even though you'll have already passed the technical interviews.

How should I prepare for Team Matching?

Team matching is an opportunity for you to choose between different teams according to your goals and preferences. Here are some things to prepare before entering team matching:

  • A general idea of what types of projects you'd like to work on. Ex.: backend, frontend, data workflows, high scale, security, user facing, high collaboration, etc.
  • One or two career goals you want to work towards on your new team
  • A 1 - 3 minute summary of your career experience to share with the potential manager
  • Examples of how you like to collaborate with your team or manager
  • 2 - 3 questions that will be tie-breakers between teams. Examples:
    • What project would I be working on when I join?
    • What are the team's core working hours?
    • What is the team operational load like?

What are the steps of team matching?

Once you've passed your technical interviews, team matching will beging with this rough structure:

  1. Recruiter tells you about one or more team opportunities
  2. Recruiter sets up 30 min calls with hiring managers for one or more team opportunities
  3. Recruiter asked if you are interested in moving forward with one or more teams. If there are multiple teams, you will be asked for your preference.

What are the benefits of team matching?

  • You may have a choice between multiple teams to select what you are most interested in
  • You have more opportunities to learn about the organization and company culture
  • You have the opportunitiy to talk to the potential managers who you will be working with
  • If the position for your application was filled, you have the opportunity to match with other teams

What are the downsides of team matching?

  • Team matching can take an unpredictable amount of time (weeks to months)
  • It is possible that team matching won't find a match and you will not get an offer
  • You may not match with the job posting you applied to

Team Matching Tips

  • Always ask your recruiter if the hiring process includes team matching
  • If team matching applies, add on 2 - 3 potential extra weeks for team matching to complete
  • Prior to entering team matching, make sure you have 2 - 3 questions to ask each team to help you decide which team you prefer
  • Do not assume you will get an offer if you are in the team matching phase
